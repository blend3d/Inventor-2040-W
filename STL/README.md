All of the files that were modified or created for this Inventor 2040 W SMARS version. 
Included is a stand so my SMARS could be be programmed without launching off of my work area as I tinker with the code.
Also added is a box for transport and storage of my SMARS with the cable and stand.

For the remainder of the parts such as wheels and tracks please see: 

    https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2662828

A big thank you to Kevin Thomas the original creator of the SMARS and Kevin Mcaleer for his adaption of the Inventor 2040 W to SMARS. Both of which inspired this version.
